## Description

```
Perfect Corp's own developer documentation describes the skin score their API
shows you as "a psychological motivator", adjusted upward because consumers
prefer positive evaluations about their skin. On my face, hydration measured
45.4 and displayed 68.

Then I sat still in front of a camera for 15 minutes. My skin did not change.
My radiance score fell 9.5 points, and a tracker comparing today to last time
would have reported 10 of 12 changes as real. All 10 would have been false.

So I built Assay: it measures the instrument's own error before it measures
your skin, and only calls a change real when it beats that margin.

Live, with the full study and the maths: https://assay.jonathanandrei.com

#skincare #skintok #datascience #buildinpublic #ai
```
